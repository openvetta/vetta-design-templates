  • One column under 720pxThe poster stacks above the queue. Ink dots on a queue row drop away before the title does. The job number wraps under the wordmark instead of squeezing it.
  • Type steps down, the ghost shrinks with itDisplay goes from 76px to 46px. The pink offset goes from 5px/3px to 3px/2px so the second plate does not collide with the next line.
  • Crop marks stay, smallerArms go from 14px to about 10px and sit closer to the corner. They are still 1px soy rules. The sheet never loses them to save space.
  • Targets grow, radius does notButtons become full width at 48px. Queue rows are at least 48px. Drums, if they remain targets, grow from 22px to 36px. Corners stay at radius-xl or sharper.
  • The shell is the deviceThe phone frame is rounded because it is a phone. Everything inside the sheet stays on the print radius. A pill button does not appear just because the screen is small.
  • Press replaces hoverThere is no hover on a thumb. Active snaps the ghost to zero — the plates in register — and that is the whole touch response. No label exists only in a hover state.
  • Halftone pitch is fixedDots stay at 4px. They stay inside the poster plates. They are not enlarged, and they are not drawn behind type under 16px.
