  1. At 900px the six columns become two. The agent, the brief, and the queue span both. Files sit beside the machine. The count sits beside memory. The agent gives up its double height.
  2. At 560px the desk is one column. Order: agent, brief, files, machine, count, queue, memory. The gutter goes from 16px to 10px. Nothing scrolls sideways.
  3. Radius eases from 28px to 22px. Padding goes from 22px to 16px, so the corner does not eat the sentence. The page inset goes from 24px to 16px.
  4. Type steps down, it does not vanish. The figure goes from 56px to 44px. The brief title goes from 30px to 26px. Body stays 16px. Eyebrows stay 12px.
  5. Targets stay 44px. Send, New job, chips, fields, and every job row. The whole row is the control. Badges that are only status may be shorter, and they still use words.
  6. Hover is a fine-pointer courtesy. A precise pointer lifts a tile 2px and deepens the shadow. A coarse pointer does not lift; a pressed pill or row darkens its edge. Inside this frame, tiles do not lift.
  7. Ink does not invert on a small screen. The agent and the machine stay dark. Reduced motion stops the lift, the press scale, and the live pulse. Color still changes.
