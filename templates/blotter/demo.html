01 — Papers

Pale, rose, resin

Three equal strips on a pink-tan desk. The base is the only dark mass, and it carries light type. Resin red is the number of the formula, not a fourth paper.

02 — Tooth

Fiber, not a pattern

A fine vertical tooth sits inside each strip. A pale lip along the top edge and a short shadow put the paper on the desk. Nothing is glossy.

03 — Type

Serif for the note, grotesque for the share

The formula and the three notes are sentence-case serifs. Shares and the ledger are tracked grotesques with tabular figures. Eighteen, thirty-four, and forty-eight are the strips. The rows beneath them sum to the same hundred.

04 — Cut

An underline, not a bottle

“Cut a strip” is a line of type with a target tall enough to hit. Hover changes its color. It does not grow a cap or a dropper.
