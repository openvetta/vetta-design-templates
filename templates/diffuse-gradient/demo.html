06 — Off-style

A forecast is a different object

The left side is the look this system refuses: an acid blend, small white type on the raw mix, glossy bubbles, and a cloud with a face. The right side is the field.

Mira is the worked example. Pools, frost, and grain are the system. The six frames are the surfaces.
