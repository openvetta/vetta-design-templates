  • Stack at 860pxThe sentence and the badges leave one row and sit under the lockup. Feature columns follow, top to bottom. Nothing becomes a sideways scroller.
  • Display follows the widthAbout 230px on a wide sheet, about 64px on a phone, 50px inside this frame. The three words stay one line each.
  • Overlap tightensThe outline shift stays in ems, so it shrinks with the type and remains inside the frame.
  • Targets stay 48pxSend, Clear, and the field do not shrink. Badges stay 28px because they are status, not the control.
  • Hover leavesA coarse pointer keeps the resting width. Press on Send draws the red outline. The focus ring stays.
  • The ink field survivesThe transcript does not turn back into paper. Its commit stays paper-on-ink. Inset drops from 48px to 20px.
  • Slide stays clippedThe moving line is cut by its own rule, on a phone the same as on a desk. The document does not gain a horizontal scrollbar.
  • Still means overlappedReduced motion pins the filled words open and leaves the outline across them. The answer does not wait on the animation.
