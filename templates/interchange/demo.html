Each line has one flat color. The name beside a bar is ink, so the word does not depend on the stroke. Accent is Reed, and it is also the small mark under the active item. Danger is only a late train or a hold. Muted is a word, not a fill.

02 — Stroke

Routes are 10px, butt caps, miter joins, miter limit 2. A segment is horizontal, vertical, or 45 degrees, which means equal absolute dx and dy. Parallel routes in one corridor sit 14px center to center, so a 4px gap of paper remains between the strokes.

03 — Marks

A station is an ink tick, 2.5px by 20px, perpendicular to the route. An interchange is a hollow ring: radius 18px, a 3px ink stroke, and an opaque paper disc with the routes redrawn across it. The ring is not a logo. Quay is set in the same face as the board, with no symbol.

04 — Controls

A row is a 22 by 10 bar, a name, a platform, and a tabular time. Rows sit 8px apart. The solid button is ink on a 44px square target. The quiet one is type only. Focus is an ink ring with a paper gap, not a shadow.

06 — 390 wide

The frame is the board at a phone width. The dock is flush with the bottom of the frame: full width, 56px, square, the same paper as the screen.

  • Dock. Below 960px the header links leave. The dock is fixed to the viewport’s bottom edge, full width, 56px, square, on the same paper. It is not inset and it has no radius.
  • Stack. The columns become one: diagram and legend, next trains, trip, service note. The trip and the note sit under the trains, so a short phone shows the diagram and the first rows first.
  • Diagram. The wide drawing is replaced by the compact one. Every route, ring, and tick stays. Ash Wharf, Harbor Steps, Glass Court, Kiln Yard, and Lantern keep their ticks and lose their names. The stroke stays 10px in the figure.
  • Type. The station name goes from 32px to 28px. Times stay 22px. Body stays 16px. The 13px line is the floor for interface text.
  • Targets. The dock, the rows, and the button are at least 44px. Bars, ticks, and rings are not targets. A coarse pointer has no hover-only state. Press does not move the control.
  • Width. At 390px the inset is 16px and the compact drawing is the full column. Nothing scrolls sideways.
