04 — The Puffet family

One seam, four batches, no weather.

Puffets are Stillhour’s own clay pebbles. Neckless, armless-until-needed, and marked by a pressed crown seam. They do not borrow suns, moons, clouds, or anyone else’s face.

Family mark
Pressed seam, about 22% down, darker on the upper lip.
Proportion
