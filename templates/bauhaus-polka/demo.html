390 — statement, then the plate

  • Margin. 56px from 1200px, 48px from 960px, 28px from 720px, 16px below that. The rule stays 1px.
  • Columns. Below 960px the hero stacks: statement, plate, then the index. The attention rail moves under the bands and becomes a row. The eight-dot row stays eight across.
  • Type. The headline goes 104, then 72, then 60, then 46. Body stays 16px. Labels stay 13px, and tracking tightens below 720px so a kicker fits the margin.
  • Dots. 8, 16, 24, and 96 do not shrink. The gap stays 8px. A phone does not invent a fifth size.
  • Ledger. Below 720px the time, the status, and the numeral share a line. The sentence moves under them.
  • Composer. Below 720px the field is full width. Clear and run share the next row, each 44px tall.
  • Pointer. The invert is limited to a fine pointer. A coarse pointer gets the same invert on press. The ink focus ring still shows for a keyboard.
