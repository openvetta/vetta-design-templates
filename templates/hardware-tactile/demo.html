02 — Shape and depth

A lip, a well, a brushed plate

Depth is a machined edge you could feel. The highlight is broad and dull. Nothing is a gel, and nothing is a pillow.

03 — Controls

Find them by touch

The notch and the word under it are the same fact. Keys say their names. The field has a legend before the value.

  • The cap matches the chassis. Orange is only the notch.
  • Ticks are engraved in the ring. They do not light up.
  • The legend is the name. The line under it is the value.

07 — Narrow

The same instrument, upright

The hand frame is Latch-1 stacked. The wide page uses the same order once the bench gets narrow.

  1. Below 860px the face stacks: well, then the four encoders, then the keys. The plate stays on top.
  2. The pad becomes four columns. Run is one cell again, still the only orange key, still at least 44px tall.
  3. Titles step down to 26px. Body stays 16px. Engraved legends stay 11px and keep their tracking.
  4. Dials stay at least 58px, over the 44px touch floor. The value remains under each name.
  5. Below 640px the prompt stacks and Run spans the row. Plates and the off-style pair become one column. Swatches wrap to two.
  6. A fine pointer may lift a resting key by 1px. A coarse pointer does not. Press still seats the key.
  7. Under reduced motion the colon holds steady and the travel stops. The seated key keeps its inset fill.
  8. Nothing on this page scrolls sideways at 390px.
